Furrow is the design system for an agricultural assessment workspace: the place where a field officer, analyst or credit committee turns farm visit notes, spreadsheets and Word reports into a structured record, an assessment and, at the end, a financial decision. Everything here serves one idea — from loose paper to a decision you can defend.

The product replaces three habits: retyping information from documents, reformatting it into a template, and re-checking it by hand. The UI therefore has to feel as calm and dependable as a good ledger, while making AI help obvious, optional and traceable.

Principles

  1. The record is the hero. Chrome stays quiet (canvas, hairlines, muted labels) so the farm's data and figures carry the page. One brand action per view.
  2. Show where every value came from. Any field filled from a document or by AI carries provenance: the file, the page, the cell. A value without a source is a value someone typed.
  3. AI proposes, people decide. AI output is always visibly suggested (ai colour, sparkle icon, the word "Suggested") until a person accepts, edits or rejects it. AI never submits, approves or declines.
  4. One path, visible. Every record moves through the same four stages — Capture → Structure → Assess → Decide. The stepper is always on screen; people always know what's left.
  5. Built for long sessions. Dense but breathable: 14px body, 36px controls, 4px grid, tabular figures. Works in bright field light (light theme) and late-night committee prep (dark theme).

Voice and content

  • Plain, specific, calm. Say what happened and what to do next: "3 fields need a source" not "Oops! Something went wrong."
  • Sentence case everywhere — buttons, titles, tabs. Verbs first on buttons: "Import documents", "Accept suggestion", "Send to committee".
  • Use the domain's words: hectares, herd size, offtake agreement, collateral, season. Don't make users translate.
  • Numbers: South African formatting by default — R 1 248 500.00, thin-space thousands, ha for hectares, dates 16 Nov 2026. Always in the mono family so columns align.
  • AI copy is modest and attributable: "Suggested from Farm_Visit_Report.docx, p. 3 · 82% confidence". Never "AI thinks…" or anthropomorphic phrasing.
  • Decisions are stated, not softened: Approved, Declined, Referred, Needs information — each with the reason in one line.

Visual foundations

Colour. A warm, paper-like neutral base (canvas, surface, surface-sunken) with deep ink. brand (field green) is for primary actions, selection and navigation. harvest (ochre) is identity and data-viz emphasis only — never text on light surfaces. ai (dew teal) is reserved exclusively for AI-produced content, so people learn to spot it at a glance. Status colours — positive, caution, negative, info — each have a -tint for backgrounds and always appear with an icon or word, never colour alone. Every text token passes 4.5:1 on the surfaces its usage note names, in both themes.

Type. IBM Plex Sans for UI, IBM Plex Mono for figures (amounts, hectares, IDs). Both are free on Google Fonts; load with:

<link rel="stylesheet" href="https://fonts.googleapis.com/css2?family=IBM+Plex+Mono:wght@400;500&family=IBM+Plex+Sans:wght@400;500;600&display=swap">

Scale: display 32 → heading-1 24 → heading-2 18 → heading-3 15 → body 14 → label 13 → caption 12 → overline 11. Figures are right-aligned with font-variant-numeric: tabular-nums.

Space and shape. 4px grid (space-1 … space-7). Controls are control-md (36px); use control-lg (44px) for decision actions and tablet use in the field. Corners are gently rounded: radius-md on controls, radius-lg on cards and panels, radius-pill for step markers and meters. Prefer a line hairline over a shadow; shadows (shadow-overlay) are for things that float.

Focus. 2px solid focus ring with a 2px offset, on every interactive element, in both themes.

Layout: the workspace shell

A three-zone layout that mirrors the job — source, structure, help:

Zone Width Holds
Navigation sidebar sidebar (240px) Workspaces, queues (My drafts, In review, Committee), search
Record canvas fluid, max 960px content Record header + stepper, the current stage's form or table
Side panel assistant (360px), collapsible Source document viewer or the AI assistant — one at a time

The record header always shows: record name, StatusBadge, owner, last saved time, and the WorkflowStepper. Unsaved work autosaves; say "Saved 2 min ago" in caption / ink-subtle.

The four stages (product pattern)

  1. Capture — DocumentDropzone takes spreadsheets, Word docs, PDFs and photos. Each file gets an extraction status. Manual entry is always possible.
  2. Structure — the extracted values land in a template as Fields and DataTables. AI-filled values arrive as ai-tinted suggested fields with provenance; a counter in the header shows "12 suggestions to review".
  3. Assess — scoring criteria, ratios and flags. AI can draft a narrative (AISuggestion), shown as a draft block to accept, edit or discard.
  4. Decide — DecisionPanel summarises the recommendation, key figures, risks and conditions; a person records the outcome and reason. The decision is audit-logged with who, when and which suggestions were accepted.

A stage can't be completed while it has unreviewed suggestions or invalid fields; the stepper shows the count instead of a tick.

Iconography

Use Lucide outline icons: 1.5px stroke, 16px in body text and tables, 20px in navigation and empty states, coloured with currentColor so they inherit the text token. One reserved glyph: sparkles means AI — use it nowhere else. Status icons: check-circle (positive), alert-triangle (caution), x-circle (negative), info (info). No logo is included yet; until one exists, set the product name in heading-3 weight 600, ink.

Components

Previews in this system are plain HTML + components/bundle.css (class prefix fw-), so they can be ported to any framework. Included: Button, Field, AISuggestion, StatusBadge, WorkflowStepper, DataTable, DocumentDropzone, DecisionPanel. Each folder's README says when to use it and what the consumer provides.

Don'ts

  • No ai colour on anything a person typed, and no AI content without the sparkle + "Suggested" label.
  • No red/green-only signalling — always pair with icon and word.
  • No gradients, glows or decorative illustrations in working screens.
  • Never put harvest text on light surfaces; never put plain white text on a dark-theme fill (use on-brand / on-negative).

Button

Buttons trigger one action, named verb-first in sentence case ("Import documents", "Send to committee").

  • Primary (fw-btn-primary, brand fill, on-brand text): the one action the screen exists for. At most one per view.
  • Default (fw-btn): secondary actions — Save draft, Export.
  • Quiet (fw-btn-quiet): low-emphasis actions in toolbars and dialogs (Cancel).
  • AI (fw-btn-ai, ai outline + sparkle): asks the AI to propose something — "Draft summary", "Extract from documents". It never commits anything by itself.
  • Danger (fw-btn-danger, negative fill): destructive or irreversible decisions — Decline, Delete record. Always confirm.
  • Sizes: fw-btn-sm (28px, inside tables), default 36px, fw-btn-lg (44px, decision actions and tablets in the field).

Consumer provides: the label, an optional 16px leading icon, disabled when the action isn't allowed yet (and a tooltip saying why).

StatusBadge

A one- or two-word status for a record, field or file, always with an icon so it never relies on colour.

Status Class Meaning
Draft fw-badge Being captured; not submitted
In review fw-badge-brand With an analyst
Needs information fw-badge-caution Blocked on the applicant or field officer
Referred fw-badge-info Escalated to committee or specialist
Approved fw-badge-positive Decision recorded: approved
Declined fw-badge-negative Decision recorded: declined
Suggested fw-badge-ai AI-produced, awaiting a person

Use the fixed vocabulary above; don't invent new statuses per screen. Consumer provides the status key; the badge picks label, icon and tone.

Field

A labelled input with help text — the unit of structured capture.

States: - Default — typed by a person. - Suggested (fw-field-ai) — filled by extraction or AI. ai-tint background, dashed ai border, a "Suggested" badge in the label and provenance in the help line (file · page or cell · confidence). Editing the value or pressing Enter accepts it and the field returns to default; the provenance stays in the audit log. - Error (fw-field-error) — negative-tint background and a help line that says how to fix it. - Read-only (fw-field-readonly) — generated or locked values on surface-sunken.

Figures (amounts, hectares, herd counts, IDs) add fw-num: mono, tabular, right-aligned, with units in the value.

Consumer provides: label, value, help text, and for suggested fields the source {file, location, confidence}.

AISuggestion

A block of AI-proposed content — a narrative, a classification, a set of extracted values — that a person must accept, edit or discard.

  • Always ai-tint + dashed ai border + sparkle + a title starting "Suggested…". The dashed border is the "not yet yours" signal; it becomes solid content once accepted.
  • Always show provenance (which documents) and confidence. Low confidence (< 60%) shows the meter in caution and the copy "Low confidence — check the source".
  • Actions in this order: Discard (quiet), Edit (default), Accept (primary). Accepting records who accepted and when.
  • Never auto-accept, never stack more than three suggestions unreviewed on screen; group the rest behind "12 more suggestions".

Consumer provides: title, body, sources, confidence (0–1), and the three handlers.

WorkflowStepper

The record's progress through Capture → Structure → Assess → Decide. Always visible in the record header.

  • Done: brand-tint disc with a check. Current: solid brand disc, ink label, aria-current="step". Upcoming: outlined disc, ink-muted label.
  • A stage with open work shows a count badge instead of letting people move on: AI suggestions to review (fw-badge-ai) or invalid fields (fw-badge-negative).
  • Steps are clickable to revisit; moving forward past a blocked stage is not allowed.

Consumer provides: the stage list, current index and per-stage counts.

DataTable

Rows of structured records — the replacement for the spreadsheet. Editable inline, with provenance per row.

  • Header on surface-sunken, caption-sized ink-muted labels; rows on surface with line hairlines; hover surface-hover.
  • Figures are fw-num (mono, right-aligned, units in the value). Never centre numbers.
  • Cell states: suggested (fw-cell-ai) and invalid (fw-cell-error) — paired with a badge in the Source column so state isn't colour-only.
  • A Source column is standard: file and cell/page, "Typed by ", or a Suggested badge.
  • Keep rows 36px (control-md); use fw-btn-sm for row actions.

Consumer provides: columns (key, label, numeric?), rows, per-cell state and source.

DocumentDropzone

Where Capture starts: people bring the documents they already have, and extraction turns them into fields.

  • Dashed line-strong drop area on surface; the browse button is always present for keyboard and touch users.
  • Each file gets a row with what was found and a status: Extracting (ai), Extracted (positive), Needs attention (caution, with the fix: "Retake photo", "Password-protected").
  • Extraction never silently overwrites typed values — conflicts become suggestions in Structure.
  • Manual entry is always offered next to the zone: "or enter details manually".

Consumer provides: accepted types and size, the file list with status, and upload/retry/remove handlers.

DecisionPanel

The end of the workflow: the recommendation, the evidence behind it, and the four possible outcomes.

  • Headline figure in figure-lg (mono); facility terms in caption.
  • Three to four KPIs, each with a value and a badge comparing it to policy ("Above 1.25×"). The threshold is always stated, not just coloured.
  • Conditions as a short list. If AI drafted the rationale, it appears as an AISuggestion above the panel until accepted.
  • Outcomes, left to right: Request information (quiet), Refer to committee, Decline (danger), Approve (primary) — all fw-btn-lg. Every outcome asks for a one-line reason and is audit-logged.
  • AI never pre-selects an outcome.

Consumer provides: facility amount and terms, KPIs {label, value, policy, tone}, conditions, and outcome handlers.
